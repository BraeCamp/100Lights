Give music a visual. Add a video and its melody becomes a synced overlay — falling notes, flowing shapes, and color you can tune. Or switch to Live mode and let the room's music drive a full-screen show on your TV or projector, great for parties.

It all runs on your device: no upload, no AI. Tweak colors, fonts, and the visual style freely.
